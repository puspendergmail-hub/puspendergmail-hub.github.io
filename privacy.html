Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

NovaPrompt ("the app" and this website) is published by Pixel Glitch Games ("we", "us"), India. This policy explains what information the NovaPrompt Android app and the website at puspendergmail-hub.github.io collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account details. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Account record. We store a small record for your account in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): your name, email, profile photo link, the time you joined and your in-app preferences.
  • Prompts you view. The prompts, titles, categories and preview images shown in the app are our own content. Reading them does not require any extra personal information.
  • Device and app information. Our service providers (Google Firebase, Google Play, and Google AdMob when ads are shown) may automatically collect technical information such as device model, Android version, app version, IP address, crash information and an advertising identifier.

2. How we use information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show you prompts and save your preferences.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (for example with Firebase App Check and Play Integrity).
  • To show ads, which help keep NovaPrompt free (see section 4).
  • To reply when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Services we use

NovaPrompt relies on the following third-party services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase App Check (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Google Play services and Google Play Integrity
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform, for ads and consent choices (policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website

4. Advertising

The app may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar technical data to show ads and measure their performance. Where required, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings under Google → Ads.

5. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers listed above, to operate the app, or when the law requires it. We do not share your personal information with other companies for their own marketing.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account record while your account is active. To delete your account and the data we hold about you, email us at the address below from the Google account you signed in with, and we will delete it within a reasonable time. Data held by Google (for example, your Google account itself) is managed by Google.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase, which applies industry-standard security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

NovaPrompt is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us.
  • You can manage ad personalisation in your device settings.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
